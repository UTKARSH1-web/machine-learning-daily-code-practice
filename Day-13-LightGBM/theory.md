# LightGBM

LightGBM stands for Light Gradient Boosting Machine.

It is a fast and efficient gradient boosting framework developed by Microsoft.

---

# Why LightGBM?

- Faster training
- Lower memory usage
- High accuracy
- Handles large datasets efficiently

---

# How LightGBM Works

Unlike traditional tree growth methods,
LightGBM grows trees leaf-wise instead of level-wise.

This often improves accuracy and speed.

---

# Important Parameters

## n_estimators

Number of trees.

## learning_rate

Controls contribution of each tree.

## max_depth

Maximum depth of tree.

---

# Advantages

- Very fast
- Memory efficient
- Excellent performance

---

# Disadvantages

- Can overfit on small datasets
- More parameters to tune

---

# Applications

- Fraud Detection
- Credit Risk Analysis
- Recommendation Systems
- Ranking Systems

---

# Conclusion

LightGBM is one of the most powerful gradient boosting frameworks and is widely used in industry.
