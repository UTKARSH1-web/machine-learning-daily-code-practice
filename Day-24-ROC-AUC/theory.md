# ROC Curve & AUC Score

ROC = Receiver Operating Characteristic

AUC = Area Under Curve

---

# ROC Curve

ROC Curve plots:

True Positive Rate (TPR)

vs

False Positive Rate (FPR)

at different thresholds.

---

# TPR (Recall)

Formula:

TP / (TP + FN)

---

# FPR

Formula:

FP / (FP + TN)

---

# Ideal ROC Curve

Top Left Corner

Meaning:

High TPR
Low FPR

Perfect Classification

---

# AUC Score

Measures area under ROC curve.

Range:

0 → 1

---

# Interpretation

AUC = 1.0

Perfect Model

---

AUC = 0.9

Excellent Model

---

AUC = 0.8

Good Model

---

AUC = 0.7

Fair Model

---

AUC = 0.5

Random Guessing

---

# Advantages

- Threshold Independent
- Useful for Model Comparison
- Widely Used

---

# Applications

- Fraud Detection
- Medical Diagnosis
- Spam Detection
- Credit Scoring

---

# Conclusion

Higher AUC means better classification performance.
